Child Safety Standards

Effective date: 01/10/2026

Ziro-Hit (package com.zirohit), provided by Zirohit Socials Pvt. Ltd., has zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE), including child sexual abuse material (CSAM). These are the standards we apply and how you can report a concern.

What is prohibited

  • Any content that sexualises, exploits or endangers a person under 18, in any form: photos, videos, text, audio, links, usernames or profile information.
  • Grooming, soliciting, or trying to contact minors for sexual purposes.
  • Sharing, requesting, trading or advertising CSAM, or links to it.
  • Using the app to arrange, promote or threaten any of the above.

Accounts that break these rules are permanently removed.

Who can use Ziro-Hit

Ziro-Hit is for people aged 18 and over.

How you can report

  • In the app: use the report option on a post, a profile or a chat to flag content or an account. You can also block an account in Settings, and you can see your past reports in the app.
  • By email: write to admin@zirohit.com. Include a link or username if you have one. Please do not forward the material itself.

Our commitments

  • We comply with the child-safety laws of the countries where we operate.
  • We keep a moderation process, in-app reporting and blocking tools available to every user.
  • We cooperate with law-enforcement requests that are lawful and valid.
