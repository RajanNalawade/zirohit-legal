Delete your Ziro-Hit account

This page explains how to delete your Ziro-Hit account (app: Ziro-Hit, package com.zirohit) and what happens to your data afterwards.

Delete it in the app

  1. Open Ziro-Hit and sign in.
  2. Open Settings.
  3. Choose Delete Account.
  4. Read the notice, enter your password, and confirm.

Deletion cannot be undone. When it succeeds you are signed out on that device.

Can't open the app?

Email YOUR-SUPPORT-admin@zirohit.com from the address registered on the account, with the subject "Delete my account". We will confirm the request and delete the account.

What is deleted

All of the account's posts and data are deleted 30 days after you confirm.

DataWhat happens
Profile, posts, comments and other content you createdDeleted after 30 days
