Delete your Ziro-Hit data

You can remove some of your data and keep your account (app: Ziro-Hit, package com.zirohit).

Delete a post

  1. Open your post.
  2. Tap the ⋯ menu.
  3. Choose Delete and confirm.

Delete a comment

Open the comment menu on a comment you wrote and choose Delete. Replies under it are removed with it.

Sign out a device

In Settings → Devices you can sign out any device that is logged in to your account.

Anything else

To delete data the app does not let you remove yourself, email YOUR-SUPPORT-admin@zirohit.com from the address registered on the account and say what you want deleted. [Add how long this takes.]
