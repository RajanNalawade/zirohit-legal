Effective date: 01/10/2026

This policy explains what information the Ziro-Hit app (package com.zirohit, "Ziro-Hit", "we", "us") collects, why, who it is shared with, and the choices you have. It is provided by [company or developer name].

1. Information we collect

2. How we use it

  • To create and run your account, and to show your posts, messages and profile to the people you choose.
  • To deliver your feed, search, notifications and chat.
  • To suggest people and content, including from your contacts and location when you have allowed them.
  • To show advertising and sponsored posts in your feed, which our servers deliver. We do not read your device's advertising ID.
  • To keep the service secure, prevent abuse and fraud, enforce our rules and handle reports.
  • To diagnose crashes, improve the app and provide support.
  • To meet legal obligations.

3. Permissions the app asks for

Each permission is optional and is requested when you use the feature that needs it. You can change them at any time in your phone's settings.

  • Camera and microphone – to take photos and record videos with sound, and for voice commands.
  • Photos and videos – to show your recent photos and videos in the post composer so you can choose what to post. You can also choose files with the system photo picker.
  • Contacts – to suggest people you may know.
  • Location – to find nearby people.
  • Notifications – to tell you about likes, comments, follows and messages.

4. Who we share information with

  • Other users. Content you post and your public profile are visible according to your settings. Messages go to the people you send them to.
  • Service providers that host our servers, send push notifications, and report crashes. We use Google Firebase for push notifications and crash reporting.
  • Advertisers and partners receive aggregate results about their campaigns, not your personal details.
  • Authorities and others when the law requires it, or to protect the rights, safety and security of our users and the service.

We do not sell your personal information.

5. How long we keep it

We keep your information while your account is active. When you delete your account, your posts and data are deleted after 30 days. Information we must keep to meet a legal obligation is kept only as long as that obligation requires: [state what and for how long, or remove this sentence].

6. Your choices and rights

  • Edit your profile and privacy settings in the app.
  • Delete individual posts and comments, and sign out any device, from the app.
  • Delete your account in Settings → Delete Account. See how to delete your account and how to delete some of your data.
  • Turn off permissions and notifications in your phone's settings.
  • Ask us to access, correct or delete your information by writing to the address below.

7. Security

We use industry-standard measures to protect your information, including encrypted connections between the app and our servers. No system is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Children

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. When we make an important change we will update the effective date above and tell you in the app.
